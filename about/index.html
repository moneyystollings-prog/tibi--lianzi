ABOUT THE JOURNAL

把好文字，留在手边。

提笔，是一份慢慢积累的个人练字手记。Between the Lines is a personal journal that grows one handwritten page at a time.

阅读与练字 / Reading & writing

每日内容围绕一个主题，中文为练字正文，英文逐句辅助理解。原文、意译和原创均在相应位置标明；来源链接随选句保留。

Each edition follows a theme. Chinese is the practice text, with line-by-line English support. Quotations, translations and original writing are labelled and linked to their sources.

写作保存 / Saving your words

手记保存在当前设备、当前浏览器的网站存储中，不会上传到网站服务器。清除网站数据、使用无痕模式或更换浏览器可能使手记无法恢复，请及时导出备份。不同设备之间不自动同步。

Writing stays in this browser on this device and is not uploaded to the website server. Clearing site data, private browsing or switching browsers may remove access to it. Export backups; devices do not sync automatically.

图片与海报 / Images & posters

文学配图根据作品全文或注明的阅读范围策划，由 AI 生成。电影主题封面为本站原创编辑设计，使用抽象的时钟、树影与星光，不使用官方海报、电影剧照或演员肖像。作品名称与短引用于介绍和评读，相关权利归原权利人。

Literary illustrations are AI-generated from documented readings. Original editorial film covers use abstract clocks, leaves and starlight rather than official key art, film stills or actor likenesses. Work titles and brief quotations support commentary; their original rights remain with their owners.
