02诗里山河 A LANDSCAPE IN VERSE

山居秋暝

唐 · 王维 / Wang Wei / An Autumn Evening in the Mountains

空山新雨后，天气晚来秋。

After fresh rain in the quiet hills, evening brings the cool of autumn.

明月松间照，清泉石上流。

The bright moon shines between the pines; a clear spring flows over stones.

竹喧归浣女，莲动下渔舟。

Voices stir the bamboo as the washerwomen return; lotus leaves move as a fishing boat glides downstream.

随意春芳歇，王孙自可留。

Let spring's blossoms fade as they will; here I am content to stay.

中文原文，简体转写 · 英文自译 / Original Chinese, simplified script · English translated for this edition

雨后山色清明，月光与泉声相映；浣女与渔舟让幽静山林有了日常生活的温度。末句“王孙”按诗人自指理解。Moonlight and running water meet after the rain; returning workers give the quiet landscape its human warmth. The final line is read as the poet referring to himself.

03古文余韵 AN ECHO FROM THE PAST

借一庭月色。

Borrow a courtyard of moonlight.

宋 · 苏轼 / Su Shi · 记承天寺夜游A Night Walk at Chengtian Temple

庭下如积水空明，水中藻荇交横，盖竹柏影也。

The courtyard seemed filled with clear water, crossed by strands of water plants; they were shadows of bamboo and cypress.

何夜无月？

What night has no moon?

何处无竹柏？

Where are there no bamboo or cypress trees?

中文原文节选 · 英文自译 / Excerpt from the original · English translated for this edition

月色本来寻常，肯与朋友停步观看的人却不多。“闲人”也含有谪居失意，并非只有无忧无虑；“积水”则是月光的比喻。Moonlight is ordinary; the willingness to pause with a friend is rarer. The writer’s leisure also carries the sorrow of exile. The water is a metaphor for moonlight.

04纸上的温度 THE WARMTH OF WORDS

济南的冬天

老舍 / Lao She / Winter in Jinan

那点薄雪好像忽然害了羞，微微露出点粉色。

The thin snow seemed suddenly shy, showing the faintest blush of pink.

中文原文短引（复句中的独立分句）· 英文自译 / A brief original clause · English translated for this edition

一点夕阳，把薄雪写成了会害羞的生命。全文描绘济南温晴的冬天；此处是近落日时的山腰，不是清晨，也不是严寒中的雪山。Evening sunlight turns the snow into something almost alive and bashful. The essay celebrates Jinan’s mild, sunny winter; this scene is a hillside near sunset, not a frozen mountain at dawn.
