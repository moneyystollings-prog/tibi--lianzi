山居秋暝
中文原文，简体转写 · 英文自译
空山新雨后，天气晚来秋。
After fresh rain in the quiet hills, evening brings the cool of autumn.
明月松间照，清泉石上流。
The bright moon shines between the pines; a clear spring flows over stones.
竹喧归浣女，莲动下渔舟。
Voices stir the bamboo as the washerwomen return; lotus leaves move as a fishing boat glides downstream.
随意春芳歇，王孙自可留。
Let spring's blossoms fade as they will; here I am content to stay.
雨后山色清明，月光与泉声相映；浣女与渔舟让幽静山林有了日常生活的温度。末句“王孙”按诗人自指理解。
Moonlight and running water meet after the rain; returning workers give the quiet landscape its human warmth. The final line is read as the poet referring to himself.

记承天寺夜游
中文原文节选 · 英文自译
庭下如积水空明，水中藻荇交横，盖竹柏影也。
The courtyard seemed filled with clear water, crossed by strands of water plants; they were shadows of bamboo and cypress.
何夜无月？
What night has no moon?
何处无竹柏？
Where are there no bamboo or cypress trees?
月色本来寻常，肯与朋友停步观看的人却不多。“闲人”也含有谪居失意，并非只有无忧无虑；“积水”则是月光的比喻。
Moonlight is ordinary; the willingness to pause with a friend is rarer. The writer’s leisure also carries the sorrow of exile. The water is a metaphor for moonlight.

济南的冬天
中文原文短引（复句中的独立分句）· 英文自译
那点薄雪好像忽然害了羞，微微露出点粉色。
The thin snow seemed suddenly shy, showing the faintest blush of pink.
一点夕阳，把薄雪写成了会害羞的生命。全文描绘济南温晴的冬天；此处是近落日时的山腰，不是清晨，也不是严寒中的雪山。
Evening sunlight turns the snow into something almost alive and bashful. The essay celebrates Jinan’s mild, sunny winter; this scene is a hillside near sunset, not a frozen mountain at dawn.
